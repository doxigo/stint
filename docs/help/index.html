Install

Stint needs macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon, and a Teamwork.com account.

  1. Download Stint.dmg from the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Open the disk image and drag Stint onto the Applications folder.
  3. Open Stint from your Applications folder. The first time, macOS asks you to approve it. See Open Stint the first time.

Stint lives in the menu bar. It appears in the Dock only while its main window is open.

Open Stint the first time

Current builds of Stint aren't signed with an Apple Developer ID or notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. To open it:

  1. Open Stint. When macOS says it can't verify the app, click Done.
  2. Choose Apple menu › System Settings, then click Privacy & Security in the sidebar.
  3. Scroll down to Security, find the message about Stint, and click Open Anyway. The button is there for about an hour after you tried to open the app.
  4. Enter your login password, then confirm that you want to open Stint.

From then on, Stint opens like any other app. If you prefer Terminal, this removes the download quarantine instead:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Stint.app"

Only do this for a copy you downloaded from Stint's releases on GitHub.

Update

Stint keeps itself up to date. Every few hours it checks its releases on GitHub, installs a new version in the background, and restarts into it once you're away from your Mac with no timer running. Your sign-in, settings and pinned tasks are kept.

To check now, or to turn this off, open Settings and go to Updates. With Install updates automatically off, Stint only checks when you click Check Now.

Stint 0.5.4 and earlier can't update themselves: quit Stint, download the latest Stint.dmg, and drag Stint onto Applications again to replace the old copy.

After an update, macOS may ask for your login password once so Stint can read its saved sign-in. See macOS asks for your password after an update.

Uninstall

Quit Stint, then drag it from Applications to the Trash. To remove its saved sign-in, settings and files too, follow Delete your data in the privacy policy. Time you logged stays in Teamwork.

Sign in

Open Stint's window (click Open Window in the popover, or press ⌘0), then click Sign In at the bottom of the sidebar. Click Sign in with Teamwork, or open Advanced to use an API token or your own Teamwork app.

Sign in with Teamwork

  1. Click Sign in with Teamwork. macOS asks to let Stint use teamwork.com to sign in; click Continue. Teamwork's login page opens in a sign-in sheet that uses your browser's Teamwork session, so if you're already signed in on the web, approving is one click.
  2. Otherwise sign in as you do on the web: password, Google, or your company's single sign-on.
  3. Once Teamwork lets Stint in, the sheet closes by itself and Stint finishes signing in.

To stop, click Cancel in the sheet or in Stint. Stint stays signed in until you sign out.

If your copy of Stint opens Teamwork's login in a Stint window instead, sign in there; the window closes by itself. Copies without Sign in with Teamwork, such as ones you build yourself, offer the two options below.

API token (quickest)

  1. In Teamwork, open your profile and choose Edit My Details, then API & Mobile. Copy your API token.
  2. In Stint, choose API Token (under Advanced, if you see it).
  3. Enter your Teamwork site, for example acme.teamwork.com. You can also paste any address from your site.
  4. Paste the token and click Sign In.

Your API token acts as you in Teamwork, so treat it like a password. Stint keeps it in your macOS Keychain.

Your own Teamwork app

This signs you in through your browser with an app you register for your Teamwork site:

  1. Open your site's Developer Portal at https://your-site/developer and create an app.
  2. Add http://localhost:8123 as a redirect URL. You can use another port, as long as the Redirect URI in Stint is the same. stint://oauth/callback works too.
  3. Copy the client ID and client secret from the app's Credentials tab.
  4. In Stint, choose Your Own App (under Advanced, if you see it), paste the client ID and secret, and click Sign In with Your App…
  5. Your browser opens Teamwork's sign-in page. Sign in and approve the app, then return to Stint.

Stint waits up to five minutes for the browser. Click Cancel to stop waiting. The client secret is kept in your Keychain, so you don't need to enter it again.

Sign out

In Settings, choose Account, then Sign Out. This removes your sign-in from the Keychain. Pinned tasks and recent items stay on your Mac, ready for the next time you sign in to the same account. Each Teamwork site and user keeps its own.

The menu bar

Stint adds a timer to the menu bar. Click it to open the popover. Choose how it looks in Settings, under Menu Bar › Show:

StyleWhat you see while a timer runs
TimeThe running time. This is the default.
Task and timeThe task's name and the running time.
Icon onlyJust a pause or play symbol.

Turn on Show seconds for a ticking clock, and Highlight while running to color the menu bar item while a timer runs.

The popover

The popover shows one day at a time, starting with today.

  • Header: the date, and the time you've logged today against your working-day target.
  • Week strip: each day's logged total. Click a day, or press ← and →, to move between days. ⌘T returns to today.
  • Timers: your Teamwork timers. The running one has a pink dot. Point to a timer to show its Edit and Log buttons.
  • Logged: the time entries for that day. Start begins a new timer on the same task.
  • Click a task's title to open it in Teamwork. Right-click a timer or entry for more actions.

The buttons along the bottom:

  • + starts a new timer (⌘N).
  • Pinned shows your pinned tasks and tasks due today.
  • Time Summary shows today, yesterday, this week and this month, with progress toward your target.
  • Log All logs every timer to Teamwork. It reads Log Timer when there's only one.
  • Open Window opens the main window (⌘0). Its arrow has Open Teamwork in Browser, Settings… and Quit Stint.

You can also drop a Teamwork task link from your browser onto the popover to start a timer on that task.

Start a timer

Click + in the popover or press ⌘N. Your pinned tasks, tasks due today, recent work and starred projects come first.

  • Type to search your tasks and projects, or paste a Teamwork task link.
  • Choose a project to see its tasks, or right-click it and choose Start Timer on Project to track time without a task.

Stint uses Teamwork's own timers, so they're the same timers you see in Teamwork on the web. Starting a timer pauses the one that's running. A timer you start on the web shows up in Stint within about 15 seconds; press ⌘R to refresh right away.

Press ⌘↩ to pause the running timer, or resume the last one. To do this from any app, set a shortcut in Settings.

Pinned tasks

Pin the work you come back to, so it's one click away. Right-click any task, project, timer or logged entry and choose Pin.

  • With the popover open, press ⌘1 to ⌘9 to start your first nine pinned tasks.
  • Click Pinned in the popover to see them all, or open Pinned in the main window. Drag them in the main window to change their order.
  • Click Log on a pinned task to log time to it directly, without running a timer. Pick the duration and date, add notes, and choose whether it's billable.

Log time

Logging turns a timer into a time entry in Teamwork, then removes the timer.

  1. Point to a timer and click Log, or right-click it and choose Log…
  2. Check the duration. It starts at the tracked time, rounded if you've turned on rounding. You can type 1:30, 1.5h or 90m.
  3. Add or edit notes, and set Billable. For a task, you can also turn on Mark task complete.
  4. Click Log.

To log several timers at once, click Log All. Choose which timers to include, and adjust each one's duration and billable setting before you log.

Teamwork logs whole minutes, so seconds are left off, and each entry must be at least one minute. To round durations to 5, 6, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, choose Round up, Round down or Round to nearest in Settings, under Time Logs. Rounding never takes a duration down to zero, and you can still edit the result.

If your connection drops while logging, Stint can't always tell whether Teamwork saved the entry. Rather than risk a duplicate, it asks you to check Teamwork first. Then choose Entry exists. Remove timer, or No entry exists. Allow retry.

Edit tracked time

Timers

Point to a timer and click Edit, or right-click it and choose Edit Task or Notes.

  • Change… moves the timer to another task or project. Its tracked time moves with it.
  • Tracked sets the timer's time, from 0:00 to 24:00. A running timer carries on from the new time.
  • Edit the notes and the Billable setting, then click Save.

Your changes are saved to Teamwork, so the web app shows them too. If Teamwork doesn't take a time change, Stint applies it when you log, and the Edit screen tells you that Teamwork's web app still shows the original time.

To delete a timer without logging it, right-click it and choose Discard Timer…

Logged entries

Double-click an entry, or right-click it and choose Edit…, to change its duration, start time, notes or billable setting. Choose Delete… to remove it from Teamwork. Entries that Teamwork has locked can't be edited or deleted.

Idle detection

If you step away while a timer runs, Stint notices. When there's been no keyboard, mouse or trackpad input for 10 minutes, or your Mac has been asleep that long, Stint asks what to do once you're back:

  • Remove and Continue takes the time you were away off the timer and keeps it running.
  • Remove and Stop takes the time off and pauses the timer.
  • Keep Time leaves the timer as it is.

Change the wait (1 to 120 minutes), or turn this off, in Settings under Idle Time. Stint only checks how long it's been since your last input. It never records what you type or which apps you use.

Reminders

Stint can send notifications to help you keep your time complete. Turn them on or off in Settings, under Notifications. macOS asks for permission first.

  • Remind me when no timer is running (off by default): every 30 minutes on weekdays between 9:00 and 17:00, counted from when you last tracked time, until today's logged and tracked time reaches your working day. Choose Resume Last Timer or Snooze 1 Hour.
  • Alert when a timer runs over 8 hours (on by default): once per timer, in case you forgot to stop it.
  • Remind me to log timers at the end of the day (on by default): once on weekdays from 17:00 if timers are left, with Log All…

The main window

Open it with Open Window in the popover or ⌘0. The sidebar has seven pages; press ⌘1 to ⌘7 to switch between them.

  • Timers: all your timers. Drop a Teamwork task link here to start one.
  • Today and This Week: your logged entries and progress. This Week charts each day against your target; click a day's bar to jump to its entries.
  • This Month: a bar for each week against its target, split into billable and non-billable time, and a total of both. Click a week to jump to its entries.
  • Pinned: your pinned tasks, in the order you choose.
  • Starred Projects: the projects you've starred in Teamwork.
  • My Tasks: tasks assigned to you, due today, overdue, upcoming or any time.

Troubleshooting

macOS asks for your password after an update

Stint keeps your sign-in in the macOS Keychain, and macOS only lets the app that saved an item read it. Current builds aren't signed with an Apple Developer ID, so after each update macOS can't tell the new version is the same app, and asks once whether Stint may read its saved sign-in.

Enter your Mac login password and click Always Allow. If you clicked Deny, quit and reopen Stint to be asked again, or sign in again.

macOS says Stint can't be opened

Approve it once in Privacy & Security. See Open Stint the first time.

“Teamwork rejected the sign-in (401)”

Your API token was changed or revoked, or your Teamwork sign-in ended. Sign in again in Settings, under Account.

“Teamwork denied access (403)”

Your Teamwork permissions don't allow that action, for example logging time on a project you're not part of. Ask your Teamwork site administrator.

A timer from the web doesn't show up

Stint checks Teamwork every 15 seconds, and whenever your Mac wakes. Press ⌘R to refresh now.

Sign in with Teamwork shows an error

  • If the page couldn't load, check your connection (and your VPN, for single sign-on), then click Sign in with Teamwork again.
  • “Teamwork didn't accept the sign-in”: a sign-in works once, within 15 minutes. Click Sign in with Teamwork again.
  • If your company signs in with Google, Google may refuse the Stint window. Use an API token instead.

Your own app doesn't return to Stint

  • The redirect URL in your Developer Portal app must match the Redirect URI in Stint exactly, including the port.
  • If another app uses that port, pick a different one, such as http://localhost:8124, in both places.
  • Stint stops waiting after five minutes. Click Sign In with Your App… to try again.

Notifications don't appear

Check that they're turned on in Stint's Settings, and allowed in System Settings › Notifications › Stint.

The global shortcut doesn't work

If another app already uses that combination, Stint says so in Settings. Choose a different one.

Open at login doesn't stay on

Allow Stint in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions.

“Earlier log receipts couldn't be read”

Stint keeps a small record of logs in progress so it never posts the same time twice. If that record is damaged, Stint sets it aside. If a log was interrupted just before, check Teamwork for a duplicate entry.

Get help

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Tell us your macOS version, which Stint release you have, and what happened. Never include your API token or client secret.
